01

The Problem

Qatar's savers have nowhere modern to invest.

  • QAR 100–5,000 typical minimum deposit at Qatar's legacy brokerage desks, plus an in-person branch visit to open an account.
  • Clunky, dated apps — Incumbent trading apps (QNBFS, and similar bank-run platforms) carry manual NIN/KYC steps and consistently poor app-store ratings.
  • No single home for U.S. + GCC stocks — Investors juggle separate, friction-heavy accounts to hold both American names and regional exchanges like the QSE.
  • Global brokers skip Qatar entirely — IBKR and eToro are technically reachable, but neither has a Qatar entity, QAR funding rails, or local support.

05

Regulatory path

The product is built. Two QFMA licenses are the last step.

Sayal already has a working v3 app, a live landing page, and secured QCB sandbox access with a connected Alpaca sandbox integration. That's roughly 70–80% launch-ready, with two QFMA licenses as the only step left.

QFMA: Securities Marketing & Promotion

Lets us legally market and promote investment in securities to Qatar residents.

  • Capital: $27,500
  • Application: $1,375
  • Licence: $27,500
  • Renewal (3 yrs): $8,250

QFMA: Broker Agent

Lets us act as agent for a licensed broker, such as Alpaca, and handle client instructions.

  • Capital: $27,500
  • Application: $1,375
  • Licence: $27,500
  • Renewal (3 yrs): $8,250

Combined cost: $500 QFC registration plus about $58,250 in QFMA application and licence fees, alongside $55,000 in required paid-up capital held at the firm, not spent.
